I ate here. It was pretty good, but the thing that kills brew pubs for me in D.C. is the lack of growlers (they are illegal).

meh. The fish and chips was less than spectacular, but the beer was good.

I had:

  • Amber Waves
  • Bier de Garde (Ale)
  • Fuel (Stout)

The last two are seasonal and by location, I was downtown.They are severed in 10oz goblets b/c they are 8% and 9% ABV.

I give the place 3.5 mehs on a 1 to 5 meh scale.

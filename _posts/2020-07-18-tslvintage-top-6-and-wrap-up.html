Wrap Up

Phil takes home the top honors in the very first Team Serious League! He defeated Jason in the finals with his powerful RUG deck featuring 3 Wrenn and Six! He earned this excellent prize trophy to sport in his deck:

We had some really amazing door prizes from excellent community artists. By way of random drawing, Sam took home this excellent two card panorama of the Cleveland skyline by Anschutz Alterations:

Keeping with the Cleveland theme, we pulled Rob’s name for this sick alter “Wild Thing” on an original Force of Will! Check out Bobby Lupo on twitter for more good stuff.
